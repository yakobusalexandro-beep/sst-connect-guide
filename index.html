SST Connect User Guide

Internal Student Support Team Management Platform

SST Connect is an internal Student Support Team (SST) management platform designed to organize and streamline school support operations. It supports referral management, student support documentation, caseload management, team communication, support planning, and support reporting.

Important Note: SST Connect is NOT intended to replace existing school systems or databases. It serves as an internal operational tool to support SST processes, improve collaboration, and centralize student support information.
Target Roles: Principals Vice Principals SST Continuum SST Members EAL Teachers

FAST

Direct Entry Shortcut

For students who are already part of an SST member's caseload, you do not need to submit or process a referral form. The student can be added directly using the form inside Student Profile.

Main Features & Modules

Explore all available modules and features inside SST Connect.

Home / Homepage
The primary landing page when opening SST Connect. It displays total active student counts for the academic year selected on the homepage, filtered by urgency status: Total Active Students, Total WS Students, Total PS Students, and Total MS Students.
Key Purpose: Instant overview of active caseload figures and quick access shortcuts for authorized users.
Overview Dashboard
Displays analytical charts detailing active student distribution by Urgency Status (MS, PS, WS), Tier, Presenting Issues, Gender, Grade Level, WS Students, PS Students, and MS Students.
Key Purpose: High-level visual analytics for reviewing school-wide support trends and demographics.
Resources Center
Provides direct access to external tools and shared documents via web links (e.g. shared documents, calendars, spreadsheets). Includes a built-in form to add new resources by providing Name, Category, and Link.
Key Purpose: Central repository for team resources, web links, and shared operational tools.
Latest Anecdotal Records
Displays a unified live feed of the latest anecdotal records created across all active cases by all users in one central view.
Key Purpose: Cross-team visibility into recent student developments and observations.
Student Profile
Displays all active students in the SST caseload for the selected academic year, organized and grouped by grade. Users can add new students, edit existing info, and add/access ATL, Anecdotal Records, Parent Meetings, and Support Plans.
Key Purpose: Comprehensive individual student record hub and central data entry module.
PYP Consultations / Consult Records
Contains FYI student information used as discussion notes and reference material for meetings. Database originates from Excel and can be updated via Excel import or direct web form.
Key Purpose: Quick reference for meeting discussion notes and consultation history.
MS Students
Categorized student view displaying active students for the selected academic year categorized under Monitor of Support.
Key Purpose: Filtered view for regular progress monitoring cases.
WS Students
Categorized student view displaying active students for the selected academic year categorized under Well-being & Safety Support.
Key Purpose: Filtered view focusing on critical priority well-being and safety cases.
PS Students
Categorized student view displaying active students for the selected academic year categorized under Priority of Support.
Key Purpose: Filtered view focusing on focused intervention cases.
Parent Meetings
Displays a complete list of parent meeting records created by team members for their respective students.
Key Purpose: Centralized log of home-school communications and parent consultations.
Add Support Plan
A quick action shortcut on the Home page that opens the Support Plan creation form directly. Includes a student dropdown picker so users don't have to open Student Profile first.
Key Purpose: Streamlined direct entry form for drafting new support plans quickly.
Student Archive
Stores historical records for students who have been in the SST caseload across multiple academic years. Filterable by academic year and status (Active, Inactive, Graduated).
Key Purpose: Multi-year historical documentation and alumni/withdrawn student lookup.
SST Caseload Summary
Features a pie chart showing the distribution of active students managed by each registered SST member in SST Connect.
Key Purpose: Workload balance analysis across the support team.
Referral Responses (Continuum Only)
Restricted to SST Continuum. Central intake console used to review incoming referral form submissions, add triage notes, assign tiers, and allocate case managers.
Key Purpose: Centralized referral triage, tier assignment, and case manager allocation console.
My Workspace
Personalized user dashboard connected directly to the logged-in email. Filters and displays only records relevant to individual assigned workload.
Key Purpose: Individual practitioner workspace for focused daily task execution.

My Workspace Deep Dive

Personalized operational hub filtering data specifically to your login account.

My Workspace connects directly to the email account used to log into SST Connect. It automatically filters all platform data to show only what requires your direct attention.

1. My Students

Displays active students directly assigned to your email address as the case manager or support specialist.

2. My Parent Meetings

Displays parent meeting records linked specifically to your assigned students or caseload.

3. My Support Plans

Displays support plans created for or linked to your assigned caseload.

4. New Referrals

Displays newly referred cases specifically allocated to you by the SST Continuum.

💡
Key Distinction to Remember:
Student Profile shows ALL active students across the entire school for the selected academic year. In contrast, My Students inside My Workspace filters exclusively to students assigned specifically to your login email.

Important Terms & Definitions

Standardized terminology and classification definitions used across SST Connect.

Allowed Values
PG A, PG B, PYP A, PYP B, PYP 1, PYP 2, PYP 3, PYP 4, PYP 5, PYP 6
Active
Student is currently enrolled at Sekolah Ciputra.
Graduated
Student has graduated and is no longer an active student at Sekolah Ciputra.
Inactive
Student has left, been withdrawn, transferred out, or is no longer active at Sekolah Ciputra.
Assign SST
The designated SST Member responsible for managing the student/case.
Assign EAL
The designated EAL Teacher responsible for supporting the student/case.
Contact
The email address of the student's homeroom teacher (entered in Student Profile).
EAL
Standalone English as an Additional Language support.
Tier 1 / Consultations
Low-intensity support, classroom-based strategies, and initial consultations.
Tier 2
Targeted, small-group or moderate individual interventions.
Tier 3
Intensive individualized interventions and specialized support plans.
Tier 2 + EAL
Combined targeted support and English language support.
Tier 3 + EAL
Combined intensive individual support and English language support.
Learning
Academic, cognitive, or instructional support needs.
Behaviour / Social Emotional
Behavioral regulation, counseling, or social-emotional support needs.
EAL
Language acquisition and support needs.
Combined Categories
Multi-domain support categories where applicable (e.g., Learning & Behaviour/Social Emotional, Learning & EAL).
MS (Monitor of Support)
Regular monitoring of student progress and support.
PS (Priority of Support)
Focused intervention required for identified support needs.
WS (Wellbeing & Safety Support)
Critical priority involving urgent well-being, safety, or behavioral concerns.
In Class Support (IC)
Push-in support provided directly within the regular classroom.
Out of Class Support (OC)
Pull-out sessions or dedicated small-group/individual intervention outside the classroom.
In Class Support + Shadow
Push-in support combined with dedicated shadow teacher support.
Out of Class Support + Shadow
Pull-out support combined with dedicated shadow teacher support.
IC + OC
Combination of push-in and pull-out support sessions.
$IC + OC + Shadow$
Comprehensive support including push-in, pull-out, and shadow teacher assistance.
